# Citation

If you use `s3lst-ds` in research or software, please cite the
[companion paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/18/13/2263). The respective citation
information is provided in the
[`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/CoLAB-ATLANTIC/s3lst-ds/blob/main/CITATION.cff) file
of the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/CoLAB-ATLANTIC/s3lst-ds). APA and BibTeX
formats are availed below.

## APA

```text
Pereira, É., Khudinyan, M., Girão, I., Marques, B., de Miranda, V. F. V. V., Sørup, H.
J. D., Paletta, Q., & Oliveira, A. (2026). A Scale-Invariance-Based Algorithm
Application for Land Surface Temperature Downscaling in Denmark. Remote Sensing, 18.
https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18132263
```
